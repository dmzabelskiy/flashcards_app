# Design System: German Flashcards

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "München '72 Run as a Daily Plan"**

The world is Otl Aicher's Munich 1972 identity program turned into a private study instrument: a white ground, light-blue bands and fields, one saturated blue that does every job of action and state, and navy ink. It is cheerful because of the blue and strict because of the geometry: every surface is a square-cornered panel with a 1px hairline, every control has a 3px corner, every measurement of progress is a row of square cells, and every icon is a pictogram drawn on a 24-unit grid with square-capped strokes. Nothing floats, nothing glows, nothing is a pill.

Density is that of a timetable rather than a feed. Today opens with a light-blue band (heading and date left, the day meter and streak right), then one white session panel spanning the column, then task rows, with the week's figures in a right rail. Every drill afterwards reads the same way: one focal prompt on a light-blue field, answer tiles in a row beneath it, feedback attached above the next prompt. The learner reads state by position and by which square is lit, and the app stays out of the way of the German.

The direction contract's refusals hold: no pastel rounded cards, no pill buttons, no progress rings, no panel shadows, no eyebrow labels above headings. Where the stylesheets diverge from the contract (a round check disc on the task rows, a single floating shadow on the language menu and mobile drawer, a residual small kicker label in the media workspace) this file records what is shipped and marks the one thing it does not carry forward.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White dominates; light blue is the material of bands and prompt fields; one blue carries action, selection, and lit state
- Navy ink at three strengths (ink, ink-2, ink-band) does all text work; no grey
- Zero radius on panels, 3px on controls, 2px on keycaps and tags; hairlines separate, shadows do not
- Archivo, self-hosted variable, set at 112% stretch and weight 650 for headings and figures, 100% stretch and 700 for the big prompt word
- Progress is square cells (12px, 16px, 18px) in a 3px or 4px gap; the 4px track appears only for in-round question position
- Pictograms: 24-unit grid, 2-unit square-capped strokes, orthogonal and 45° lines only, always via the `#pic-…` sprite
- Motion is short (120ms hover, 260ms feedback, 300ms cell) on one easing curve and fully disabled under reduced motion

## Colors

A three-value world: white ground, a five-step light-blue scale for fields and bands, and one blue for everything that acts or is on; navy ink for text; green, red and yellow only as feedback state; three article colours as a learning device.

### Primary
- **Olympic Blue** (`blue`): the one action and state colour. Primary buttons, links, the lit meter cell, the current nav item's text, the selected tile's border and inset ring, focus outlines, the caret, the brand mark, the big completion percentage, and the highlighted span inside a drill prompt. It is never a background for text panels.
- **Olympic Blue Pressed** (`blue-700`): hover and active on primary buttons only.

### Secondary
- **Light-blue scale** (`sky-50` … `sky-300`): the material of the world, not an accent. `sky-300` is the top bar and the Today band; `sky-100` is the drill prompt field, the flashcard front, the current nav item, table headers, the info message, the selected-tile fill and the tag fill; `sky-200` is the unlit meter cell, the progress track, the focus halo and the scrollbar thumb; `sky-50` is hover on white surfaces (buttons, nav items, table rows) and the quiet example box. `sky-300` is also the text-selection colour.

### Tertiary (feedback state)
- **Result Green** (`good`) on **Green Field** (`good-bg`): correct feedback, the done task row and done session panel, the check disc, the good rating button. `good` text on `good-bg` passes 4.5:1.
- **Result Red** (`bad`) on **Red Field** (`bad-bg`): incorrect feedback, form errors, the danger button, the error message. Same contrast guarantee.
- **Caution Yellow** (`warn`) on **Yellow Field** (`warn-bg`): the "hard" rating button (ink text on yellow) and the warning message. Never text.
- **Article colours** `der` (blue), `die` (orange-red), `das` (green): carried as 14px square discs and as the colour of the article choice's accent. They are a learning device and must stay distinguishable from one another and from good/bad; note `der` shares the primary blue's value by design.

### Neutral
- **White** (`white`): page ground, every panel, every button and input at rest.
- **Ink** (`ink`): all body and heading text; the navy the world writes in.
- **Ink Quiet** (`ink-2`): secondary text, sublines, field labels, metric captions, disabled buttons.
- **Ink on Band** (`ink-band`): text set on a light-blue surface (band date, prompt instructions, table headers, keycap text) so navy stays legible against `sky-100`/`sky-300`.
- **Hairline** (`line`): the 1px border of panels, tables, rows and the gap colour of hairline grids (metrics, completion stats, verb feedback grid).
- **Hairline Strong** (`line-strong`): borders of interactive things: buttons, inputs, answer tiles, the review card, the session panel.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** A single blue (`blue`) does every job of action, selection, current state, and lit progress. No second accent, no gradient, no tint of blue used as an "on" colour except this one.
**The Light-Blue Is Material Rule.** The sky scale is a surface, never an accent: bands, fields, headers and hover fills. Text on it is `ink` or `ink-band`, never `blue` except on the current nav item.
**The State Pair Rule.** Feedback colour always ships as a pair: `good` on `good-bg`, `bad` on `bad-bg`. A pictogram (`#pic-check`, `#pic-cross`) or wording accompanies the colour; colour alone never carries state.
**The Alias Freeze Rule.** The legacy names in `:root` (`--primary`, `--accent`, `--mint`, `--surface`, `--surface-2`, `--border`, `--muted`, `--mono`, `--font-mono`, `--danger`, `--easy`, `--hard`, `--article-*`, …) exist only so unrebuilt selectors resolve. New CSS uses the München names above and nothing else.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (variable, self-hosted, SIL OFL; fallback system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo (same file)
**Label/Mono Font:** none; there is no monospace face. Figures use Archivo with `tabular-nums`.

**Character:** One grotesk in two widths. Headings and figures are Archivo stretched to 112% at weight 650 with -0.02em tracking, which gives the signage voice of the Munich program; the single big prompt word is set at normal width, weight 700, -0.03em, so the German word reads as the object of study rather than as a heading. Body is regular weight at 16px/1.5. Italic (the second variable file) marks example sentences only.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(3rem, 7vw, 4.5rem)`, line-height 1, stretch 100%, -0.03em): the drill prompt word on the light-blue field; the flashcard question scales to `clamp(2.7rem, 8vw, 5rem)` with the same setting; the completion percentage goes to `clamp(4.5rem, 14vw, 7.5rem)` at stretch 112% and -0.05em.
- **Headline** (650, 1.875rem, 1.15, stretch 112%, -0.02em): page `h1` and the Today band heading (2.25rem there). Drill title bars use 1.5rem.
- **Title** (650, 1.25rem, 1.15, stretch 112%): `h2` section headings; the session panel heading is 1.375rem, rail and drill-card headings 1.125rem, `h3` 1rem.
- **Figure** (650, 1.75rem, 1.1, stretch 112%, tabular-nums): the number in a metric cell, rail stat, or deck count; article letters in answer tiles are 2.25rem at 700.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.5): everything else; answer text in the review card is 1.35rem, typed inputs and tile text 1.05rem.
- **Label** (600, 0.8125rem, 1.25): field captions above inputs and tiles, table headers, metric captions, `small`, helptext, tags and sublines at 0.875rem. Sentence case; no letter-spacing; never uppercase.

### Named Rules
**The Wide Heading Rule.** Anything that behaves as a heading or a figure is Archivo at stretch 112%, weight 650, -0.02em. Anything that is the German word being studied is stretch 100%, weight 700, -0.03em, and as large as the field allows.
**The No-Eyebrow Rule.** Headings carry no small label above them. Context goes below the heading as a subline in `ink-2` at 0.875rem (`.drill-subline`), or nowhere.
**The Tabular Figure Rule.** Every number that can change (counts, scores, dates, streaks) is set with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so meters and rows do not shift.

## Layout

The shell is a light-blue top bar (56px min, `sky-300`) followed by the three-stripe signature (three 2px rows: blue, `sky-300`, `sky-100`), then a two-column grid of a 232px white sidebar and the main column, 32px gap, 1280px max, 24px page padding. The sidebar is sticky at 16px, collapses to 64px (labels hidden, pictograms centred), and below 860px becomes a fixed drawer that slides in over a navy scrim. Content pages inside the main column use a 1120px container with 32px/24px padding and 64px bottom.

Today is a `minmax(0,1fr) 300px` grid with a 24px gap: band, then session panel, then task rows in the left column; four figures and seven activity bars in the rail. Drill workspaces are a single 840px column; the typed-study review is 820px; completion and the flashcard memory mode 760px; forms 760px; card detail 640px; the delete confirmation 420px.

Spacing rhythm is a 4-based scale: 1px hairline grids (`gap: 1px` with `line` as the background), 3px and 4px meter gaps, 8px between tiles and rows, 10px–14px between form controls and inside rows, 16px–18px panel padding, 20px–24px between sections, 32px–40px inside prompt fields and review cards. Panels butt against each other with 8px–10px gaps, not margins of air.

Breakpoints in use: 860px (shell to drawer, Today to one column), 780px (drill grids stack, article tiles become rows, tables become card lists, `h1` clamps), 700px (verb form stacks), 640px (band and rows stack on Today), 520px (completion stats stack, tighter tiles), 420px (brand name and language value hide). A short-viewport rule (`max-height: 720px`, desktop) tightens drill vertical rhythm so prompt, tiles and feedback fit one screen.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth is expressed by material, not by shadow: white panels sit on the white ground separated by a 1px `line` hairline; the light-blue field is lower than the white panel; the `sky-300` band is the strongest surface. Hairline grids (metrics, completion stats, verb feedback) are built by a 1px `line` gap between white cells, not by borders on each cell. Selection is an inset ring (`inset 0 0 0 1px blue` on tiles), and focus is a 2px `sky-200` halo on inputs or a 2px blue outline elsewhere.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.14)`, token `--shadow-float`): only for things that genuinely leave the page: the language menu popover, the mobile navigation drawer, and the theater overlay controls. Never on a panel, card, button or field at rest.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline Not Shadow Rule.** A surface is separated from its neighbour by a 1px `line` or `line-strong` border, or by a change of material (white / sky-100 / sky-300), never by a shadow.
**The Float Only Floats Rule.** `--shadow-float` appears only on layers that overlay the page (popover, drawer, theater). If it is on something in the document flow, it is wrong.

## Shapes

Squares and hairlines. Panels, bands, fields, meter cells, heatmap cells, activity bars, article discs, the brand mark and the stripes all have 0 radius. Interactive controls (buttons, inputs, selects, answer tiles, nav items, message boxes, feedback asides, the example box, the result pill) have a 3px corner (`--radius`) so they read as pressable without becoming pills. Keycaps and tags have 2px. The only circles in the world are the pictograms' own circles and the 24px check disc on Today's task rows (`border-radius: 50%`, 2px `line-strong` ring, filled `good` when done), which is the checkbox idiom.

Borders are 1px: `line` on containers and tables, `line-strong` on controls and on the panels the learner acts in (session panel, review card, flashcard back). Nothing is clipped or masked; the only overflow rule is the 4px progress track.

Pictograms follow the sprite's drawing rules: a 24×24 viewBox, 2-unit strokes, `stroke-linecap: square`, `stroke-linejoin: miter`, `fill: none`, `stroke: currentColor`, lines orthogonal or at 45° only, plus circles and rectangles. Sizes: 16px (`pic--sm`, stroke 2.25), 22px (default), 32px (`pic--lg`, stroke 1.75), 44px (`pic--xl`, stroke 1.5); 18px inside buttons; 20px in the speaker button. Always `<svg class="pic"><use href="#pic-…"></use></svg>`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 3px corner (`--radius`), 44px minimum height, 9px 18px padding, weight 600, 8px gap to an 18px pictogram; 48px and 52px minimums on the session Start button and drill submits.
- **Primary** (`.button.primary`, `.btn-primary`): `blue` fill and border, white text; hover `blue-700`. One per view: Start studying, Check, Sign in, Retry.
- **Secondary / Ghost** (`.button`, `.btn`, `.btn-ghost`): white fill, `line-strong` border, ink text; hover `sky-50`. Row actions, Dashboard, Show answer.
- **Rating** (`.good`, `.hard`, `.easy`, `.danger`): `good` white-on-green, `warn` ink-on-yellow, `easy` same as primary, `danger` white-on-red; each hover darkens by hand (#125a36, #e2b30f, #9e2f17).
- **Hover / Focus:** background, border-colour and colour transition 120ms on `--ease`; focus is the global 2px blue outline at 2px offset. Disabled: `ink-2` text at 0.6 opacity, no fill change.
- **Link button** (`.link-btn`, `.drill-exit`): no border, transparent, ink at 600; on the band hovers to 55% white. Speaker button (`.tts-button`): 44px square, white, `line-strong`, blue pictogram.

### Chips
- **Tag** (`.tag`): `sky-100` fill, `blue` text, 0.75rem 600, 2px 8px padding, 2px corner. Names the day's drill inside a row title.
- **Answer tag** (`.answer-tag`): white fill, `line-strong` border, 3px corner, weight 650; shows the answered or expected form inside feedback.
- **Keycap** (`kbd`): 22px `sky-100` square with `ink-band` 0.75rem text and 2px corner; theater keycap is a transparent 1px currentColor box.

### Cards / Containers

- **Background:** white for panels (`.card`, `.hero`, `.row`, `.widget`, `.form`, `.drill-answer-panel`, `.review-card`, `.card-detail`, `.confirm-panel`); `sky-100` for fields (`.drill-prompt-panel`, `.completion-panel`, `.flashcard-panel`, flip-card front); `sky-300` for the Today band; `good-bg` with a transparent border for a done row or done session panel.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px `line` on containers, `line-strong` on the session panel, review card and flashcard back.
- **Internal Padding:** rows 13px 18px; widgets 16px 18px; answer panels 18px; session panel 22px 24px; forms 24px; fields 40px 32px (prompt), 44px 32px (completion), 48px 36px (flashcard); review card 40px 36px. Mobile halves the outer padding to roughly 20px.
- **Hairline grid** (`.metrics`, `.completion-stats`, `.verb-feedback-grid`): `gap: 1px` on a `line` background, white cells, 14px 16px padding, figure at 1.75rem.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white, 1px `line-strong`, 3px corner, 44px min height, 8px 12px padding, inherits body type; 52px and 1.05rem in drill and typed-study answer forms. - **Focus:** border becomes `blue`, 2px `sky-200` halo, no outline; 120ms.
- **Error / Disabled:** errorlist and `.form-error` in `bad` at 0.8125rem; helptext `ink-2`.

### Answer tiles
- **Style:** white, `line-strong`, 3px corner, 52px min height, weight 650; article tiles are 140px-tall columns with a 14px square article disc, a 2.25rem letter and a keycap, collapsing to 76px rows below 780px.
- **State:** hover `sky-50`; checked `blue` border, `sky-100` fill, 1px inset blue ring; graded correct/incorrect recolour the ring and fill to the `good`/`bad` pair; focus is the global blue outline on the label's box.

### Navigation

### Square-cell meters (signature)
Progress is a row of squares. `.meter` is a wrapping flex row with a 3px gap; each `.meter-cell` is a 12px `sky-200` square that becomes `blue` when `.is-on` (200ms). Variants: `.meter--lg` 16px cells and 4px gap (completion, 20 cells); `.meter--day` 18px cells in 70% white on the band (three cells, one per task); `.meter--session` 20 cells whose lit cells play `cellOn` (300ms scale from 0.6, staggered 28ms per cell via `--i`). The 4px `.progress-track` (`sky-200` with a `blue` span) is used only for question position within a round, never for goals or scores. The stats heatmap (12px cells, 3px gap, five sky-to-blue levels) and the activity week (bars on a `sky-50` track, today's bar `blue`) are the same square idiom.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build every new surface from white panels with 1px `line` hairlines and 0 radius, with `sky-100` for the one field the learner focuses on and `sky-300` only for a page's band.
- **Do** use `blue` for the single primary action on a view and for every "on" state (current, selected, lit); hover darkens it to `blue-700` and nothing else changes.
- **Do** set headings and figures in Archivo at `font-stretch: 112%`, weight 650, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`, and the studied German word at stretch 100%, weight 700, -0.03em.
- **Do** show progress as `.meter` square cells (12px/16px/18px, 3px–4px gap), and reserve the 4px `.progress-track` for question position inside a round.
- **Do** draw new icons in the `_pictograms.html` sprite on the 24-unit grid with 2-unit square-capped strokes and only orthogonal or 45° lines, and place them with `<svg class="pic"><use href="#pic-…">`.
- **Do** ship feedback as the `good`/`good-bg` or `bad`/`bad-bg` pair with a check or cross pictogram and words, and keep der/die/das as 14px square discs in their three colours.
- **Do** keep the 44px minimum on every control, the 2px blue focus outline, `aria-live` on feedback, 120ms `--ease` hover transitions, and the reduced-motion kill switch.
- **Do** put page context below the heading as an `ink-2` 0.875rem subline, and set changing numbers in `tabular-nums`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a radius above 3px to anything, or use pill buttons, rounded slabs or progress rings; the only circle is the task-row check disc.
- **Don't** put a shadow on a panel, card, button or field; `--shadow-float` is for the language popover, the mobile drawer and theater overlays only.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent, a gradient, a grey text colour, or a tint of blue as an "on" state; the palette is the tokens in the frontmatter and nothing else.
- **Don't** write eyebrow or kicker labels above headings, uppercase tracked labels, or a monospace face; none exist in this world.
- **Don't** use the legacy aliases (`--primary`, `--accent`, `--mint`, `--surface*`, `--border*`, `--muted`, `--mono`, `--font-mono`, `--danger*`, `--easy`, `--hard`, `--article-*`) in new CSS; they are compatibility shims for unrebuilt selectors.
- **Don't** use glyph icons, emoji, icon fonts or inline one-off SVGs; every icon comes from the pictogram sprite.
- **Don't** let colour alone carry correct/incorrect or article identity.
